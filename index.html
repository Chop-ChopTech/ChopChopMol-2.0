Features

Everything You Need for Molecular Work

From simple visualization to complex simulations, ChopChopMol provides professional-grade tools accessible from your browser.

3D Visualization

GPU-accelerated instanced rendering with ball-and-stick, space-filling, wireframe, and cartoon styles. Smooth arcball controls with perspective and orthographic cameras.

Selection & Editing

Click, box-select, or select by chain and residue. Replace atoms, define rotation axes, create fragments, and edit structures interactively.

Measurements

Measure bond distances, angles, and dihedral angles with precision. Real-time calculations with persistent labels as you interact with structures.

Trajectory Playback

Animate multi-frame trajectories from MD simulations, geometry optimizations, or torsion scans. Scrub through frames with energy tracking.

Cloud & Remote Files

Save molecules to Firebase cloud storage. Connect to remote servers via SSH/SFTP to browse, open, and save files on HPC clusters.
